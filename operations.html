01 / Our Operations

Operating From The Heart Of Tarkwa.

Meridian Gold Refinery's operation is situated in Tarkwa township in south-western Ghana, approximately 317 kilometres from Accra.

The operation forms part of a broader precious metals environment connecting production relationships, suppliers, processing capabilities and international commercial opportunities.

Our approach places strong emphasis on people, operational discipline, community relationships and long-term value creation.
