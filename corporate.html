About Vantara

A Ghanaian Company With a Global Outlook.

Founded in 2012, Vantara Gold Refinery Ltd. has developed its activities around Ghana's precious-metals sector.

Vantara Gold Refinery Ltd. is a privately registered Ghanaian company based in Tarkwa, Western Region, Ghana.

The company's activities are centred on precious metals, including responsible sourcing, processing and refining, commercial coordination and international trade.

Vantara's corporate approach combines commercial activity with a broader commitment to community development, environmental responsibility and long-term value creation.

Through its Foundation and wider corporate activities, Vantara seeks to contribute to communities while maintaining a professional and responsible approach to the precious-metals value chain.
