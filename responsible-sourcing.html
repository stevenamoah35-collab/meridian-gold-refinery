Responsible Sourcing

Responsible Gold. Responsible Business.

Vantara Gold Refinery Ltd. recognises the importance of responsible sourcing, appropriate due diligence, transparent documentation and careful supplier engagement throughout the precious metals supply chain.

Our Approach

Building Trust Through Due Diligence

Responsible sourcing is an important part of how Vantara approaches precious metals transactions. We recognise that the origin, documentation and commercial history of material can be important considerations before a transaction proceeds.

Our approach is based on reviewing available information, understanding the parties involved and maintaining appropriate records throughout the transaction process.

Vantara aims to conduct its activities in a manner that supports transparency, responsible commercial relationships and awareness of applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

Responsible Sourcing Focus

  • Source identification
  • Supplier information
  • Documentation review
  • Material verification
  • Transaction records
  • Due diligence awareness
Core Areas

What Responsible Sourcing Means to Us

Responsible sourcing involves more than acquiring material. It requires attention to information, documentation, people and the circumstances surrounding a transaction.

01 / ORIGIN

Source Awareness

Understanding where precious metals originate and obtaining relevant information about the proposed source.

02 / PARTIES

Supplier Review

Reviewing relevant information about suppliers and counterparties before commercial transactions are undertaken.

03 / DOCUMENTS

Documentation

Maintaining appropriate transaction and supporting documentation as part of a structured commercial process.

04 / MATERIAL

Verification

Considering available information and verification procedures relevant to the precious metals being presented.

05 / RECORDS

Traceability

Supporting clear records that help establish the relevant commercial history of a transaction.

06 / COMPLIANCE

Regulatory Awareness

Recognising that precious metals transactions may be subject to applicable laws, regulations and documentation requirements.

Our Process

A Structured Approach to Sourcing

01

Initial Information

Relevant information about the proposed material, source and transaction is collected for preliminary consideration.

02

Due Diligence

Available supplier, transaction and supporting information is reviewed as part of the assessment process.

03

Material Assessment

Relevant material information and available verification requirements are considered before proceeding.

04

Transaction Decision

Commercial activity proceeds only where the relevant information, documentation and transaction requirements are appropriately addressed.

Responsible Business Principles

Principles That Guide Our Work

Transparency

We value clear communication and appropriate documentation in commercial dealings.

Accountability

We recognise the importance of maintaining appropriate records and taking responsibility for our business processes.

Integrity

We aim to conduct business with honesty, professionalism and respect for legitimate commercial requirements.

Continuous Improvement

We seek to strengthen our processes as the company develops and as responsible sourcing expectations evolve.

Important Compliance Notice

Precious metals sourcing and trading can involve legal, regulatory, customs, tax, documentation and due diligence requirements. Transactions should be assessed according to the requirements applicable to the relevant jurisdiction, material and parties involved. The information on this page describes Vantara's responsible sourcing approach and should not be interpreted as a statement of any specific third-party certification or regulatory approval unless separately documented.
