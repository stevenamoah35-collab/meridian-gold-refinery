Operations

Structured Operations. Long-Term Value.

Tarkwa, Western Region, Ghana

The operation is situated in the Tarkwa township in south-western Ghana, approximately 317 kilometres from Accra. The operation employs more than 680 people, with 74% from host communities.

Community Development

Creating Value Beyond The Mine.

Meridian Gold Refinery Ghana Foundation

The Community Development Fund was established in 2012 for the Tarkwa and Tamso host communities and was formally registered the same year as the Meridian Gold Refinery Ghana Foundation.

The Foundation focuses on education, health, water and sanitation, agriculture and infrastructure.
