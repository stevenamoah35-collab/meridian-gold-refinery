Our Services

Integrated Precious Metals Services.

Vantara Gold Refinery Ltd. provides a structured range of precious-metals services covering assaying, refining, smelting, vaulting, transportation and export.

What We Offer

Services Across the Gold Value Chain.

Our service model is designed to support the movement of precious metals from assessment and processing through secure storage, logistics and international market access.

01 — ASSAY

Assaying

Assaying is a fundamental part of precious-metals processing. It determines the precious-metal content and supports valuation, processing decisions and quality control.

  • Gold content assessment
  • Sample analysis
  • Fire assay
  • XRF analysis
  • Trace-element analysis where applicable
02 — REFINING

Refining

Gold refining involves controlled processing designed to separate gold from other metals and impurities and produce refined precious-metal products.

  • Initial purification
  • Chemical refining processes
  • Electrolytic refining where applicable
  • Purity verification
  • Melting and casting
03 — SMELTING

Smelting

Smelting involves controlled high-temperature processing of gold-bearing materials to separate valuable metals from unwanted materials before further processing.

  • Material preparation
  • Controlled furnace processing
  • Flux management
  • Molten-metal handling
  • Casting and further processing
04 — VAULTING

Vaulting

Secure precious-metal storage supports the integrity of the supply chain by protecting bullion and maintaining controlled inventory management.

  • Secure bullion storage
  • Controlled access
  • Inventory management
  • Collection and delivery coordination
  • Secure handling procedures
05 — TRANSPORT

Transport

Precious metals require carefully controlled logistics. Transportation arrangements can be coordinated to support the secure movement of bullion between approved locations.

  • Secure logistics coordination
  • Shipment preparation
  • Route planning
  • Chain-of-custody procedures
  • Airport transfer coordination
06 — EXPORT

Export

Export services support the movement of processed precious metals into international markets through appropriate documentation, logistics and regulatory procedures.

  • Export documentation
  • Regulatory coordination
  • Logistics management
  • International shipment coordination
  • Destination arrangements

Quality, Transparency & Responsibility

Vantara Gold Refinery Ltd. aims to maintain disciplined processes, responsible business practices and appropriate quality controls throughout the precious-metals value chain. Specific services, facilities and technical capabilities are subject to applicable regulatory requirements and operational availability.
