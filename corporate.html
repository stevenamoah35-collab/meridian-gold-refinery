About Vantara

Built Around Precious Metals

Vantara Gold Refinery Ltd. operates as an independent company within Ghana's precious metals sector, with a focus on creating structured value across sourcing, processing, refining and international commercial activity.

Our corporate approach is centred on professionalism, responsible business practices, careful transactions and long-term development within the precious metals industry.

As a privately owned company, Vantara maintains its own corporate identity and operates independently in pursuit of its business objectives.
