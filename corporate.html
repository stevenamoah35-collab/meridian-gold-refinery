Meridian Gold Refinery is a Ghanaian precious metals company based in Tarkwa, Western Region, Ghana, operating across the precious metals value chain.

01 / Company Profile

Connecting Ghana's Resources With Global Opportunity.

Operating since 2007, Meridian Gold Refinery participates across the precious metals value chain, including gold production relationships, sourcing, refining relationships and international trade.

The company works with gold producers, suppliers, refining facilities and international trading partners to support commercial connections between Ghanaian mineral resources and international markets.

Our objective is to create sustainable commercial value through strong relationships, responsible business practices and long-term participation in the precious metals sector.
