Suppliers

Building Reliable Supply Channels.

Vantara Gold Refinery Ltd. welcomes enquiries from legitimate suppliers and commercial parties involved in the precious metals sector.

Supplier Engagement

Professional Sourcing Starts With Trust.

Vantara is interested in establishing clear and professional commercial relationships with appropriate suppliers of precious metals.

Supplier enquiries may be reviewed based on the nature of the material, available documentation, source information and the requirements applicable to the proposed transaction.

Our approach places importance on transparency, appropriate verification and responsible commercial conduct.

Supplier Focus

  • Precious metals enquiries
  • Source information
  • Supplier identification
  • Documentation review
  • Material information
  • Transaction assessment
Supplier Requirements

Information That Helps Us Understand Your Offer

Providing clear and relevant information helps establish whether a proposed supply arrangement can be properly considered.

01 / SUPPLIER

Supplier Details

Provide relevant information identifying the supplier or commercial entity making the enquiry.

02 / MATERIAL

Material Information

Describe the type, form and relevant characteristics of the precious metals being offered.

03 / ORIGIN

Source Information

Provide available information concerning the origin and commercial history of the material.

04 / DOCUMENTS

Supporting Documents

Relevant documentation may be requested to support the review of a proposed transaction.

05 / QUANTITY

Commercial Details

Where appropriate, provide information about quantity, timing and proposed commercial terms.

06 / CONTACT

Direct Communication

Clear contact information allows the relevant enquiry to be reviewed and followed up efficiently.

Supplier Process

From Initial Enquiry to Commercial Review

01

Initial Enquiry

The supplier provides basic information about the proposed material and commercial opportunity.

02

Information Review

Available supplier, source, material and transaction information is reviewed.

03

Verification & Due Diligence

Additional information or documentation may be requested where necessary for appropriate assessment.

04

Commercial Discussion

Where the enquiry is suitable, the relevant commercial details can be discussed with the appropriate parties.

Documentation

Clear Information Supports Good Transactions.

The specific documents required can vary depending on the material, transaction and applicable requirements.

Supplier Identification

Appropriate information identifying the supplier or commercial entity may be required.

Source Documentation

Relevant information concerning the source and origin of material may be requested.

Transaction Records

Appropriate records may be maintained in relation to commercial discussions and transactions.

Verification Information

Additional information may be requested where necessary to assess the proposed transaction.

Important Supplier Notice

Supplier enquiries are subject to review and do not automatically create a purchase commitment or commercial agreement. Transactions may be subject to applicable laws, regulations, documentation, verification, due diligence and other requirements relevant to the material and parties involved.
