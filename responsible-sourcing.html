RESPONSIBLE SOURCING

Responsible Gold.
Responsible Business.

We recognize that responsible sourcing extends beyond the transaction. It requires attention to ethical conduct, supply-chain relationships, social responsibility and environmental considerations.

OUR APPROACH

Responsibility Across The Value Chain.

Meridian Gold Refinery works with producers, suppliers, refining facilities and commercial partners within the precious metals value chain.

We seek relationships that support professional standards, dependable business practices and appropriate compliance with applicable requirements.

Responsible sourcing is viewed as an ongoing business principle rather than a single stage of the purchasing process.

OUR COMMITMENT

Build commercial relationships that recognize the importance of integrity, accountability, social responsibility and environmental responsibility.
