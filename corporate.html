Meridian Gold Refinery operates within Ghana's precious metals value chain, connecting production, sourcing, refining relationships and international trade through structured commercial activities.

WHO WE ARE

A Ghanaian Precious Metals Enterprise.

Meridian Gold Refinery is based in Tarkwa, Western Region, Ghana, and has operated since 2007.

The company participates across the precious metals value chain, working with gold producers, suppliers, refining facilities and international trading partners.

Our objective is to connect Ghana's mineral resources with international markets while creating sustainable commercial value through professional relationships.

Our Business Focus

Gold production, sourcing, refining relationships and international precious metals trade form key areas of our commercial activities.
