Responsible Gold. Responsible Business.

Building responsible commercial relationships across the precious metals value chain through integrity, accountability, respect and long-term value creation.

Our Approach

Responsibility Across The Value Chain.

Responsible sourcing is an important part of sustainable precious metals business. Meridian Gold Refinery works with producers, suppliers, processing relationships and trading partners while supporting responsible commercial practices.

01

Integrity

We value honest, transparent and professional relationships throughout the precious metals value chain.

02

Accountability

Appropriate documentation, communication and responsible processes support dependable commercial relationships.

03

Respect

We recognise the importance of people, communities, suppliers and stakeholders connected to our activities.

Responsible Value Chain

From Source To Market.

Responsible business practices should remain part of every stage of the commercial journey.

Source

Build appropriate sourcing relationships and support responsible supply practices.

Supplier

Work with dependable partners that value integrity, accountability and responsible conduct.

Processing

Connect precious metals with appropriate processing and refining relationships.

Trade

Support professional commercial transactions and international market relationships.

Market

Create sustainable commercial value through long-term relationships and responsible business practices.

The Community Development Fund was established in 2012 for the Tarkwa and Tamso host communities and was formally registered the same year as the Meridian Gold Refinery Ghana Foundation.

The Foundation focuses on development priorities that support communities connected to the company's operating environment.
