Gold Sourcing

Responsible Gold. Reliable Sources.

Vantara Gold Refinery Ltd. approaches gold sourcing with a focus on responsible commercial practices, source verification, documentation and long-term value within the precious metals sector.

Our Sourcing Approach

Connecting Ghanaian Gold With Legitimate Markets

Gold sourcing is an important part of the precious metals value chain. Vantara Gold Refinery Ltd. seeks to work through appropriate commercial channels when evaluating gold supply opportunities.

Our approach places importance on understanding the source of material, reviewing relevant documentation and considering the circumstances surrounding each transaction before proceeding.

Source With Purpose

Responsible sourcing supports confidence, transparency and accountability across the precious metals supply chain.

Sourcing Standards

What We Consider

Each sourcing opportunity can involve different commercial, documentary and verification requirements. Our framework is designed to support careful consideration before transactions proceed.

SOURCE

Source Information

Understanding the origin and nature of gold offered for purchase is an important part of responsible sourcing.

SUPPLIER

Supplier Information

Relevant supplier and transaction information may be reviewed to establish the commercial context of a proposed transaction.

DOCUMENTS

Documentation

Appropriate records and supporting documentation help establish the details of material and its proposed movement.

VERIFICATION

Material Verification

Gold may be subject to appropriate verification and assessment before further processing or commercial activity.

COMPLIANCE

Due Diligence

Relevant legal, regulatory and transaction requirements should be considered before a sourcing arrangement proceeds.

TRACEABILITY

Record Keeping

Clear records support accountability and help maintain a structured precious metals supply chain.

Sourcing Process

From Opportunity to Transaction

A structured process helps Vantara evaluate sourcing opportunities before material enters subsequent stages of the precious metals value chain.

Initial Review

Review the proposed source, supplier information and basic transaction details.

Due Diligence

Consider relevant documentation, verification requirements and applicable transaction conditions.

Assessment

Assess the material and supporting information using appropriate procedures.

Transaction

Proceed with appropriate commercial arrangements where the relevant requirements have been satisfied.

Responsible Sourcing

Principles That Guide Our Approach

Responsible sourcing is an ongoing process that requires attention to the source, documentation, people and circumstances connected with precious metals transactions.

Transparency

We value clear information and appropriate documentation throughout sourcing activities.

Accountability

Structured records and appropriate checks help support responsible commercial decision-making.

Traceability

Understanding the movement and relevant history of material is an important part of supply-chain responsibility.

Responsible Business

We seek to conduct our activities with professionalism and respect for applicable requirements.
