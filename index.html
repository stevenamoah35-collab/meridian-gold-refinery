01 / Corporate Profile

Built Around Ghana's Precious Metals Economy.

Operating since 2007, Meridian Gold Refinery participates across the precious metals value chain, with a focus on gold production relationships, sourcing, refining connections and international trade.

Our objective is to connect Ghana's mineral resources with international markets while creating sustainable commercial value through strong relationships and responsible business practices.
