ABOUT MERIDIAN

Built Around Ghana's Precious Metals Opportunity.

Meridian Gold Refinery operates from Tarkwa in Ghana's Western Region and has been operating since 2007.

The company participates across the precious metals value chain, working with producers, suppliers, refining relationships and international trading partners.

Our objective is to connect Ghanaian mineral resources with international markets while creating sustainable commercial value and long-term relationships.
